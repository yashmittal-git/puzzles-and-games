Push-up alarm demo

Circuit build · 3 Nov 2026 · inspired by the PushClock reel Rathish shared

Pick a rep target and press start. The buzzer rings until the camera counts your push-ups - every full rep flies the bird through the pipes. Hit the target and the alarm stops.

Demo, not a real alarm. It works only while this page is open and the screen is on - it cannot wake a locked phone. Prop the phone so the front camera sees your arms and shoulders, side or front. Front-on counting is a new fix - if a rep is missed, use the tap button and tell us. Counting runs on-device; no video leaves the phone.
