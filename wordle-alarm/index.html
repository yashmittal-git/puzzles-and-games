Daily word alarm demo

Circuit build · unofficial Wordle-style game · a new word every day at midnight IST

Press start. The buzzer rings until you solve today's word. Six guesses, green/yellow/gray, same rules as the real thing. The word changes every day at midnight IST, picked from our own built-in list.

Demo, not a real alarm. It works only while this page is open and the screen is on - it cannot wake a locked phone. This is our own word game, not the official NYT Wordle; each daily word is independently selected from our own list and is not synced with NYT. Everything runs on this device; nothing is uploaded.
